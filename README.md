# Awesome-Food-Safety-n-Haccp

## Top Food Safety & HACCP Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on HACCP Plans, Digital Checklists, Temperature Monitoring, Audits, Corrective Actions & Food-Safety Compliance*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Food Safety & HACCP**. These systems help restaurants, manufacturers, and foodservice operators create HACCP plans, record temperature and hygiene checks, manage corrective actions, run audits, and maintain compliance evidence.



**Examples** include Safefood 360°, FoodDocs, Jolt, Trail App, Checkit, HACCP Now, ComplianceMate, SmartSense, MeazureUp, and SafeCheck (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full digital HACCP and multi-site food-safety platforms are mostly commercial. Practical open options include local/self-hosted HACCP and traceability tools, general checklist/form engines, and related food-data projects. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the commercial gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Safefood 360°](https://safefood360.com/)**  

  Enterprise food-safety management system covering HACCP plans, audits, corrective actions, documents, supplier controls, and multi-site compliance for manufacturers and processors.



- **[FoodDocs](https://www.fooddocs.com/)**  

  User-friendly HACCP and food-safety platform that auto-generates plans, SOPs, monitoring tasks, and compliance records for restaurants and food businesses.



- **[Jolt](https://www.jolt.com/)**  

  Operations and food-safety platform popular with restaurants for digital checklists, temperature logs, opening/closing routines, and team accountability.



- **[Trail App](https://www.trailapp.com/)**  

  Food-safety and operations checklist platform used by multi-unit operators for daily monitoring and compliance documentation.



- **[Checkit](https://www.checkit.net/)**  

  Connected workplace and food-safety platform combining digital checklists, sensor monitoring, and automated alerts for temperature and process control.



- **[HACCP Now](https://www.haccpnow.co.uk/)**  

  Digital HACCP and food-safety management software focused on plan creation, monitoring records, and compliance for food businesses.



- **[ComplianceMate](https://compliancemate.com/)**  

  IoT-enabled food-safety platform offering real-time temperature monitoring, automated logs, and compliance dashboards.



- **[SmartSense](https://www.smartsense.co/)**  

  Environmental and food-safety monitoring platform using sensors and analytics for temperature, humidity, and condition tracking across facilities.



- **[MeazureUp](https://www.meazureup.com/)**  

  Restaurant operations and food-safety platform for checklists, audits, and multi-location performance tracking.



- **[SafeCheck and related digital HACCP tools](https://www.example.com/)**  

  Additional platforms focused on mobile checklists, sensor integration, and audit-ready food-safety records.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Haccp Trace and local HACCP / traceability tools](https://haccp.alessandrostella.it/)**  

  Self-hosted / local open tools for food traceability, lot tracking, cold-chain records, and basic HACCP-oriented documentation (especially useful for smaller EU operators).



- **[General digital checklist and form open engines](https://github.com/)**  

  Self-hosted form and inspection platforms (e.g., community forks of open survey/checklist tools) that can be configured for temperature logs, cleaning schedules, and hygiene checks.



- **[SafetyCulture-style open inspection alternatives](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects aiming to provide offline-capable inspections, photo evidence, and corrective-action workflows similar to commercial audit apps.



- **[Temperature and IoT open collectors](https://github.com/)**  

  Open firmware and backends for logging sensor data (temperature, humidity) that can feed food-safety records.



- **[Recipe, allergen, and kitchen open management tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Self-hosted recipe and kitchen systems that support allergen tracking and basic process documentation.



- **[Food recall and hazard open data explorers](https://github.com/jonathanhild/foodsafetyexplorer)**  

  Dashboards and pipelines for analysing public food-safety recall data to support risk awareness.



- **[Lab and QC open platforms (adjacent)](https://github.com/)**  

  Open quality-control tools (e.g., Westgard-style monitoring) that share audit-trail and lot-tracking concepts useful in food labs.



- **[Document control and SOP open repositories](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple open systems for versioning HACCP plans, SOPs, and training records.



- **[Corrective-action and CAPA open workflows](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open issue-tracking configurations adapted for non-conformances and corrective actions.



- **[Documentation and HACCP open playbooks](https://github.com/)**  

  Community guides and templates for building digital monitoring programs aligned with Codex HACCP principles.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Starting with self-hosted checklists and sensor logs for single-site or small multi-site operators.

- Using open form engines + shared storage to replace paper temperature and cleaning logs.

- Accepting that automated HACCP plan generation, multi-site dashboards, certified sensor ecosystems, auditor-ready evidence packs, and large-scale compliance reporting still favor commercial platforms (Safefood 360°, FoodDocs, Jolt, Checkit, ComplianceMate, SmartSense, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, offline capability, and lower cost for independent operators.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Define critical control points and monitoring schedules → capture checks via open mobile forms or sensors → store records with timestamps and photos → trigger simple corrective-action workflows → export evidence for audits. Suitable for small operators and internal tool teams. Most multi-unit and manufacturing food businesses rely on commercial food-safety platforms for consistency and audit defensibility.
